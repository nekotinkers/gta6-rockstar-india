# Indian-origin names in the GTA VI credits

A one-page breakdown of the people with Indian-origin names in the official **Grand Theft Auto VI** credits: how many there are, which departments and teams they work in, and what roles they hold.

**Live site:** [GTA6.NEKOTIN.WORLD](https://gta6.nekotin.world/)

## Highlights

- **2,204 people** with likely Indian-origin names, holding 2,258 credit lines
- **19.8%** of the 11,146 unique names in the complete credits list
- Largest shares by department: Outsourced Services (74.0%), Animation (62.6%), Art (42.0%) and Quality Assurance (33.2%)
- 213 people hold a lead, principal or director-level title

## What's on the page

- Headline stats
- People per department, split by confidence, with each department's percentage
- Breakdown by seniority, from director to associate
- The 15 largest teams
- A searchable, filterable table of every person with their department, team and role

## Method

Data comes from the official credits page at [rockstargames.com/VI/credits](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/credits), read on 19 November 2026.

The credits don't list nationality or studio, so names were identified from surnames and given names typical of Indian languages and regions, then checked by hand:

- **High confidence:** clearly Indian names
- **Medium confidence:** South Asian names that are equally common in neighbouring countries, Goan and Mangalorean Christian names, and diaspora cast members

Song credits and company names are excluded from all totals. A name alone can't confirm anyone's nationality or heritage, so treat every number as an estimate. Some people will be missed, and a few may be miscounted.

## Running it

It's a single static `index.html` with the data built in. It has no dependencies and no build step, and makes no external requests. Open it in a browser from the [provided url](https://gta6.nekotin.world), or host it with GitHub Pages:

1. Put `index.html` in the root of the repo.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, then `main` and `/ (root)`.
