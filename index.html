Method and caveats

Source: the official credits page at rockstargames.com/VI/credits, read on 19 November 2026. Once song credits and company names are taken out, the full list has 12,196 credit lines and 11,146 unique names. Band and artist names in the soundtrack section are still counted. The credits don't say where anyone lives or which studio they work at, so this list is built from names alone: surnames and given names typical of Indian languages and regions, then checked by hand.

"High" means the name is clearly Indian (for example Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Punjabi or Northeast Indian names). "Medium" covers names that are South Asian but just as common in Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal or Sri Lanka, Goan or Mangalorean Christian names, and diaspora cast members in the US or UK. Clearly Sri Lankan names were left out. A name says nothing certain about someone's nationality or heritage, so every count here is an estimate: some people will be missed and a few will be miscounted.

Seniority comes from the job title: anything with "Lead" counts as Lead, "Principal" as Principal, and so on. All names are as published in the official credits.
